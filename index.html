Easy Paneer Wraps

By Chef: Jyoti Suri (JS)

Enjoy soft wraps filled with lightly spiced paneer, colourful vegetables, and a creamy yogurt sauce. This simple vegetarian recipe is perfect for lunch or a quick dinner.

Big flavour, Simple Ingredients, Homemade Comfort.

  • Preparation time: 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: 10 minutes
  • Servings: 4 wraps

Ingredients:

Quantity Ingredient
250g Paneer, cut into small cubes
4 Medium flour tortillas
1 tablespoon Cooking oil
1 medium Onion, thinly sliced
1 medium Bell pepper, thinly sliced
½ teaspoon Ground cumin
½ teaspoon Ground coriander
¼ teaspoon Turmeric
¼ teaspoon Red chilli powder
½ teaspoon, or to taste Salt
1 teaspoon Lemon juice
1 cup Shredded lettuce
½ cup Plain yogurt
1 tablespoon Finely chopped fresh mint
1 small Garlic clove, finely grated

Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients:
    Cut the paneer into small cubes. Slice the onion and bell pepper, then wash and shred the lettuce.
  2. Make the yogurt sauce:
    In a small bowl, combine the yogurt, mint, and garlic. Mix well and set aside.
  3. Cook the vegetables:
    Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper. Cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly softened.
  4. Add the spices:
    Add the cumin, coriander, turmeric, chilli powder, and salt. Stir for about 30 seconds to coat the vegetables.
  5. Cook the paneer:
    Add the paneer cubes and gently stir them into the vegetables. Cook for 3–4 minutes until the paneer is heated through.
  6. Finish the filling:
    Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice.
  7. Warm the tortillas:
    Heat each tortilla in a separate dry frying pan for about 15–20 seconds on each side, until soft and warm.
  8. Assemble the wraps:
    Spread some yogurt sauce over each tortilla. Add lettuce and divide the paneer filling evenly between the four tortillas.
  9. Fold and serve:
    Fold the sides inward, then roll each tortilla tightly around the filling. Cut in half and serve immediately.

Tips and Best Practices

  • Cut the paneer into similar-sized cubes so it heats evenly.
  • Avoid cooking the paneer for too long, as it can become firm.
  • Reduce or leave out the chilli powder for a milder filling.
  • Warm the tortillas before folding to help prevent tearing.
  • Avoid overfilling the wraps so they are easier to roll.
  • Assemble just before serving to keep the lettuce crisp and the tortillas from becoming soggy.
